Our Story

Amaya Built Co. LLC was built by two brothers, Hector and Eduardo Amaya, who grew up around concrete and construction their entire lives. Inspired by their father’s work ethic as a concrete foreman in Mexico, they learned early on the importance of quality work, honesty, and taking pride in their craft.

With backgrounds rooted in both hands-on concrete work and construction management, Hector and Eduardo founded Amaya Built Co. with one goal — to deliver quality work, honest service, and projects built to last.

Today, Amaya Built Co. proudly serves communities across Nebraska by delivering clean, durable, and professional concrete work customers can rely on.

What We Believe

At Amaya Built Co. LLC, we believe customers deserve a company they can trust to deliver quality work at a fair price. We value honesty and communication to ensure every client gets exactly what they envision for their project. We also believe in helping improve and strengthen the communities we serve. Most importantly, safety is the foundation of our company — making sure everyone returns home safely the same way they arrived on the job site.
